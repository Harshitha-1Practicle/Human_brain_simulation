🧠 Major Brain Regions & Functions

👩‍💼

Frontal Lobe

Involved in planning, decision-making, reasoning and speech production.

🖐️

Parietal Lobe

Processes touch, pain, temperature and helps with spatial awareness.

🎧

Temporal Lobe

Supports hearing, language understanding, memory and emotion.

👁️

Occipital Lobe

Handles visual processing, object recognition and motion detection.

🤸

Cerebellum

Coordinates movement, balance, posture and precision during action.

❤️

Brainstem

Controls breathing, heartbeat, alertness and basic survival functions.

🧠

Hippocampus

Helps form new memories and connects experiences with context.

⚡

Thalamus

Relays sensory information before it reaches higher brain regions.

🧩

Amygdala

Processes emotions and helps detect threats or social signals.

🏃

Motor Cortex

Plans and executes voluntary muscle movement across the body.

🗣️

Broca Area

Helps produce fluent speech and language expression.

📖

Wernicke Area

Helps understand spoken and written language meaning.
