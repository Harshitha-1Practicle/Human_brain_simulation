Meet the brain's major regions

Each region has a distinct role, but the brain works as a connected network. Use these cards as a quick anatomy guide, then return to the dashboard to explore the animated model.

👩‍💼

Frontal Lobe

Planning, reasoning, decision-making, personality and voluntary movement.

🖐️

Parietal Lobe

Touch, pain, temperature and the body's sense of space.

🎧

Temporal Lobe

Hearing, language comprehension, memory and recognition.

👁️

Occipital Lobe

Visual information, color, shapes, motion and recognition.

🤸

Cerebellum

Balance, posture, coordination and motor learning.

❤️

Brainstem

Breathing, heart rate, alertness and vital reflexes.
