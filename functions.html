Memory

The hippocampus helps create new memories, while connected cortical networks store and retrieve knowledge.

🏃

Movement

The motor cortex plans voluntary actions and the cerebellum refines balance, timing and precision.

🗣️

Language

Distributed language networks help us understand words, choose meaning and produce speech.

🎨

Vision

Visual pathways transform light into information about edges, color, movement and objects.

💛

Emotion

Emotion networks evaluate experiences, prepare the body to respond and shape memory.

Brain circuits coordinate sleep, wakefulness and daily biological rhythms.
